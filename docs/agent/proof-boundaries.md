# Proof Boundaries

ProofLayer exists to keep career outputs traceable to reviewed evidence. These semantic boundaries are permanent unless the product explicitly introduces a stricter reviewed contract.

## Factual Foundation

- Reviewed evidence is the factual foundation for candidate claims and public outputs.
- A target title describes desired positioning. It is not employment history and does not prove seniority.
- A project is not employment unless it has been explicitly reviewed as employment evidence.
- A responsibility is not an achievement or outcome.
- A technical capability, domain, or tool may be used only within the scope supported by reviewed evidence.
- Metrics and quantified outcomes require reviewed, approved `verified_metric` support.
- Do not invent or strengthen seniority, authority, organizational scope, adoption, scale, team size, dates, employers, technologies, performance, revenue, users, or outcomes.

## Human And Model Boundaries

- Model output is always a proposal and is never automatically approved.
- Human-edited wording receives the same schema, provenance, privacy, and factual validation as accepted model wording.
- Approval makes no model call.
- Rendering and export make no model call.
- A model may reorganize or propose wording only within the IDs, evidence, claim boundaries, and metric permissions supplied to it.
- Rejected, excluded, blocked, stale, invalid, or unreviewed material must not cross into approved output.

## Output Boundaries

- Rendering may change presentation, ordering, layout, and supported formatting, but not the approved meaning.
- Visible resumes, websites, and exports must not expose private provenance, internal IDs, local paths, secret values, or private source content.
- Public wording must respect visibility and approved wording overrides.
- Role-based positioning and job-specific application workflows remain distinct. A Role Target is not a Job Target, and job-specific inputs must not silently alter reusable role artifacts.
- Job descriptions and target profiles are external targeting inputs, not candidate evidence.
